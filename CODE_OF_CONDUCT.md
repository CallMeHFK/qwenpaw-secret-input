# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on the code and the design, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before attributing malice.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's credentials, logs, or identifying
  information. Security reports go through the private channel in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Posting real secrets, tokens, or personal data in issues, pull requests, or
  commit messages.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors whose
behaviour violates this code. To report a concern, use GitHub's **Report abuse**
flow or contact the repository owner privately; reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from the
project by the repository owner.
